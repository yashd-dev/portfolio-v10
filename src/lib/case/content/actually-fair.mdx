<Section id="overview" title="Overview">
	<Lead>
		Actually Fair is a shopping site built around one idea: the price you see should make sense.
	</Lead>
	<P>
		The store sells across athleisure, backpacks and travel gear, sunglasses, jewelry, electronics,

		inventory and cash on delivery.

		I work on it as technical lead, end to end: the headless Shopify backend, the Next.js
		storefront, and the custom services around them.
	</P>

<Section id="problem" title="The problem">
	<P>
		Online stores often keep pricing and sourcing opaque, and the shopper has no way to tell what
		anything should actually cost.
	</P>
	<P>
		Actually Fair's bet is that a store can win on the opposite: show fair pricing plainly, on every
		product, and let that be the brand.
	</P>
</Section>

<Section id="storefront" title="The storefront">

		each with its own landing view: athleisure, backpacks and travel, sunglasses, jewelry,
		electronics, and skincare, with new arrivals surfaced per category.
	</P>
	<Fig

<Section id="pricing" title="Pricing in the open">
	<Pullquote>transparent pricing on every product. no, for real.</Pullquote>
	<P>
		That line is the store's whole pitch, and the design treats it as a contract rather than a
		slogan. Product cards show the selling price next to the reference price with the percentage
		off, and product pages keep the same math in view instead of hiding it behind a sale badge.
	</P>
</Section>

<Section id="checkout" title="Cart and checkout">

		pattern.

<Section id="wish" title="Make a wish">
